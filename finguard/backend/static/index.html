Why datasets were combined

FinGuard fuses two public financial datasets that are normally used in isolation:

  • creditcard.csv — 284,807 real, anonymized card transactions with confirmed fraud labels (492 frauds, 0.17%). Powers the core detection model.
  • marketing_campaign.csv — 2,240 customers' income, spend and channel behavior. Powers the persona segmentation layer.

Since these are separate public datasets, this demo assigns each simulated transaction a persona synthetically to illustrate the mechanism — in production, personas would be learned per real customer from their own transaction history.
